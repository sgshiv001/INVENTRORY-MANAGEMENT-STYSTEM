MCA ACADEMIC PROJECT

Inventory control made simple, clear, and practical.

InvenTrack demonstrates how a browser-based information system can organize product records, monitor stock, manage suppliers, and maintain an auditable history of inventory changes.

HTML5CSS3JavaScriptNode.jsSQLite
ITv2.0

Project objective

Replace error-prone manual stock registers with a responsive system that provides fast search, validation, low-stock visibility, and useful reports.

Core modules

  • Product catalogue
  • Reorder planning
  • Stock movement ledger
  • Supplier management
03

Data model

Related records are connected with unique IDs, while SKU validation prevents duplicate product codes.
